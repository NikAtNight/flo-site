Privacy

Speech recognition runs on your Mac. Here's what stays local and what uses the network.

Your voice
Held in memory while it's transcribed. Nothing is written to disk unless you turn on diagnostic recordings.
Your words
Pasted where you're typing and logged to a daily Markdown file on your Mac. You can switch the log off.
Account, analytics
No sign-in and no telemetry.
Speech model
Downloaded on first launch and cached. After that, dictation works offline.
Update check
Signed updates through Sparkle, checked daily. You can turn it off in Settings.
Writing tools

Edit by talking

Select text and use command mode to ask for a rewrite. "Make this shorter." "Turn this into bullets." With nothing selected, it writes at the cursor. Command mode uses Apple Intelligence on macOS 26.

Formatting by voice

Say "new paragraph," "bullet point," or "thumbs up emoji." Spoken ordinals make numbered lists. Long pauses start new paragraphs.

Vocabulary and corrections

Correct a misheard word and Flo applies the fix from then on. Whisper also accepts custom vocabulary for names and jargon.

Snippets

Say a trigger phrase and Flo replaces it with your saved text.

App-aware cleanup

With cleanup on, text is casual in Messages, proper in Mail, and preserves camelCase in editors.

Your clipboard survives

Flo pastes, then restores whatever you had copied. Password fields use typed keystrokes instead.

Cancel with Escape

Cancel while you're talking or while the text is being transcribed. Nothing gets pasted.

Speech engines

Whisper Large v3 Turbo

The default engine. Supports custom vocabulary for names and jargon.

Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3

Live text updates about once a second while you talk. No custom vocabulary.

Optional cleanup removes filler words and false starts. It runs through Apple Intelligence on macOS 26 or a local model through Ollama.
