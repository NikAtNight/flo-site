Privacy

Here's everything that leaves your Mac.

The list is short on purpose. Speech recognition runs on the Neural Engine through Core ML, so there's no server to send anything to.

  • Your voiceStaysHeld in memory while it's transcribed. Nothing is written to disk unless you turn on diagnostic recordings.
  • Your wordsStaysPasted where you're typing, and logged to a Markdown file per day on your Mac. You can switch the log off.
  • Account, analyticsNoneThere's nothing to sign in to and no telemetry.
  • Speech modelOnceDownloaded on first launch and cached. After that, dictation works offline.
  • Update checkDailySigned updates through Sparkle. You can turn it off in Settings.

Your clipboard survives

LocalFlow pastes, then puts back whatever you had copied. Password fields get typed keystrokes instead.

esccancelled

Changed your mind?

Press Escape while you're talking, or while it's still transcribing, and nothing gets pasted.

Speech engines

Two models. Both run on your Mac.

Default

Whisper Large v3 Turbo

The most accurate option. It also takes your custom vocabulary, so names and jargon come out right.

Fastest

Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3

Live text updates about once a second while you talk. No custom vocabulary.

Want cleanup that strips "um" and fixes false starts? Turn it on and it runs on Apple Intelligence (macOS 26), or on a small local model through Ollama.
